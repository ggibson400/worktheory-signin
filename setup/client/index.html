Work Theory

Add your expert to your Claude

Your expert works with you through Work Theory. Once this is set up, Claude knows where things stand with you and draws on what your expert has learned. When something should go to your expert, Claude asks them and brings back their answer.

You'll need

  • A claude.ai account of your own, on a plan with projects and connectors.
  • The email address your expert invited.
  • The passcode your expert sent you. It's yours alone; if you lose it, ask them for a new one.

1Connect Claude to Work Theory once

  1. In claude.ai, go to Settings → Connectors (under Customize) and choose Add → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Work Theory, with this URL:
    https://vykvpabpeumddbrgoxnb.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
  3. Choose Connect, sign in with your email address and your passcode, and choose Allow.
  4. Open Work Theory in your connectors and set every group under Tool permissions to Always allow, so Claude saves as it goes without stopping to ask. Nothing is ever deleted; the history stays.

2Make a project for your work with your expert

  1. Create a claude.ai project named after your business, your team or yourself.
  2. Copy these instructions into the project's instructions.

  3. Add any documents you want Claude to know: plans, figures, notes. Claude goes through them and keeps what matters.
  4. Start a conversation in the project, check Work Theory is on in the tools menu, and send:
    Check that Work Theory is connected, brief me, and tell me what we should do first.

If Claude can't find you by that name, it shows the names you're registered under, so you can pick the right one.

Building things in Claude Code optional

If you build things with Claude (a website, a tool, a script), you can do it in Claude Code, on your computer or on the web at claude.ai/code, with your expert in the loop the same way. It reaches Work Theory through the connector you added in step 1.

  1. Sign Claude Code in with the same claude.ai account. On your computer, /mcp lists Work Theory; on the web, check Work Theory is on for the session.
  2. Add this to the CLAUDE.md file at the top of the code's folder (with your name from step 2 filled in), creating the file if there isn't one. Commit it, so sessions on the web get it too.

  3. Start a session in that folder and send the same first message as in claude.ai. When Claude Code asks to use a Work Theory tool, allow it, so it saves as it goes.

On the web, a session reaches package registries and GitHub only. To let it deploy to a service such as Supabase or Vercel, add the service's domains under the environment's network access (Custom), and store its keys as network secrets, never in the code.

What to expect

  • Claude starts each conversation knowing what's been recorded about you. The first time, it may offer to take down your situation; you can skip that.
  • It saves what matters as you go (where things stand, what you decide and why, how it turns out) and tells you what it saved, so you can correct it.
  • Some questions go to your expert. Claude gives you its best answer for now and says your expert will confirm. Their answer comes back in their words, even in a conversation you already have open.
  • Your expert can ask you things too. Claude puts their questions to you, in their words, and sends back your answers.
  • Claude sees your records only, never your expert's other clients. What those clients have taught your expert reaches you as lessons with no names in them.

If Claude says Work Theory has been updated: go to Settings → Connectors → Work Theory → ⋯ → Refresh tools list, check the permissions are still Always allow, and start a new chat.
